Rilo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Rilo is a screen-time buddy app made by Ethan Dunn in Kentucky, USA ("Rilo," "we," "us"). The short version: everything Rilo knows about you stays on your iPhone. We don't collect your data, we don't have servers that store it, we never sell it, and there are no ads.

What Rilo uses, and where it stays

Things you tell Rilo

  • Your first name, goals, hobbies, your plans and tasks, and anything you type, pick, or say in the app.
  • All of this is saved only on your iPhone. We never receive it.

Your voice

  • Rilo only listens while you hold the talk button. Letting go, leaving the screen, or hitting the time limit turns the mic off.
  • Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone. The audio and text are never sent to us.

Rilo's AI

  • Rilo's replies come from Apple's built-in AI, which runs on your iPhone. Your conversations are not sent to any outside AI company.
  • If your iPhone can't run Apple's AI, Rilo uses pre-written replies instead.

Calendar (optional)

  • If you allow it, Rilo reads the calendars on your iPhone (including any you've added in iPhone Settings, like Google) to build your daily plan around your schedule. This stays on your phone. Rilo doesn't change or delete your events.

Screen Time

  • Rilo uses Apple's Screen Time system to block or limit the apps you choose and show your usage. This data is handled by Apple on your iPhone. Rilo can't read your messages, photos, or what you do inside other apps, and it's never sent to us.

Purchases

  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active.

What we collect

Nothing. Rilo has no accounts, no servers storing your data, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. The only time Rilo connects to the internet is for Apple's App Store purchases.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete Account (or Erase My Data) in Rilo, or delete the app. Since your data only lives on your iPhone, that removes all of it.

Age requirement

Rilo is for people 13 and older.

Changes to this policy

If Rilo changes how it handles data (for example, if we add accounts or cloud backup), we'll update this policy and tell you in the app before it happens. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
