Rilo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Rilo is a screen-time buddy app made by Ethan Dunn in Kentucky, USA ("Rilo," "we," "us"). This policy explains what Rilo collects, why, and how you control it. The short version: we only collect what Rilo needs to help you spend less time on your phone, we never sell your data, and there are no ads.

What we collect

Things you tell Rilo

  • Your first name, goals, hobbies, and the answers you give during onboarding.
  • What you say in check-ins with Rilo. Rilo only listens when you press and hold the talk button. It never listens in the background.
  • Plans, schedules, and tasks you create or edit.

Things you choose to connect

  • Calendar (optional): If you connect your Apple or Google calendar, Rilo reads your events to build your daily plan around them. Rilo does not change or delete your events unless you ask it to.
  • Location (optional): If you allow it, Rilo uses your approximate location to suggest things to do nearby. You can turn this off anytime in Settings.
  • Screen Time: Rilo uses Apple's Screen Time tools to block or limit the apps you choose and to show your usage. This data is handled by Apple's system on your device. Rilo cannot read your messages, photos, or what you do inside other apps, and we do not upload your Screen Time data to our servers.

Purchases

  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. Apple only tells us whether your subscription is active.

Basic app data

  • Crash reports and simple usage stats (like which screens are used) so we can fix bugs and improve Rilo. These are not used to identify you or for ads.

How we use it

  • To run Rilo's features: your plan, check-ins, limits, streaks, XP, and moods.
  • To make Rilo's replies personal to you.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with advertisers.

AI features

When you talk to Rilo or ask it to make a plan, the text of that conversation (not your Screen Time data) is sent to an AI service provider to create Rilo's reply. We use providers that do not use this data to train their AI models. Voice check-ins are turned into text before being sent.

Who else sees your data

Only the service providers Rilo needs to work (cloud hosting, the AI provider, and Apple for purchases). They can only use your data to provide their service to Rilo. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data from our servers within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete Account in the app, or email us.
  • Turn off connections: You can disconnect your calendar, location, or Screen Time permission anytime in the app or your iPhone's Settings.
  • Get a copy or fix something: Email us and we'll help.

Age requirement

Rilo is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 is using Rilo, email us and we will delete their data.

Security

We use encryption and trusted providers to protect your data. No system is perfect, so we can't promise 100% security, but we take it seriously.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
